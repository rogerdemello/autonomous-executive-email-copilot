What the service does

The copilot reads a mailbox you connect, classifies and ranks what arrives, drafts replies, checks those drafts against the message they answer, and holds every outbound action in an approval queue. It records what it did in a per-workspace audit log.

It is an assistant, not an agent acting alone. No reply, escalation, or other outbound action leaves your mailbox unless a person with the admin role or higher approves it. That gate is unconditional; it is not a setting you can switch off, and we will not add one.
