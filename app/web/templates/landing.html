CLASSIFY · SPAM

Junk never reaches you

Every message is classified as spam, normal, or urgent before anything else happens. Spam is filed with no reply and no human attention spent; the copilot never engages it, and the manufactured "urgency" of a promo email earns nothing.

  • Spam is labeled and filed automatically; it only ever adds a label.
  • The rationale is shown, so you can see exactly why it was filed.
  • Filter the inbox by classification to audit the calls it made.

DRAFT · EDIT · APPROVE

A professional reply, ready for your pen

For mail worth answering, the copilot writes the reply, fact-checks it against the source message, and waits. The draft opens in an editor; amend it freely before approving. Your edits are what get sent, and they teach the copilot your workspace's voice.

  • Model-drafted, with confidence shown next to the draft.
  • Verified against the source; invented numbers or names get flagged.
  • Nothing sends until you press "Approve & send".

Under the hood

An assistant that reads before it writes.

Deterministic routing, model-written prose, and a checker that reads every draft back against its source, assembled so every action is grounded in your mailbox, explained to you, and yours to approve.

SIGNALS

Signals before prose

Before anything is drafted, the copilot infers the sender's role, the deadline, and the risk from the message itself, and keeps the whole thread one click away, so every decision is shown beside the conversation it came from.

DECIDE

Code decides, the model writes

Priority, risk, deadline, and the choice between reply, escalate, defer, and file are computed by deterministic code that behaves identically with no model configured. The model is handed that decision, plus a few of your own approved replies as a guide to your voice, and asked only for words. An outage costs wording, not triage.

VERIFY

Drafts are fact-checked

Every draft is read back against the message it answers. A number the source never said, or a name it never mentioned, is flagged and shown right beside the approve button.

GUARD

Injection-aware by default

Incoming mail is treated as untrusted input. Prompt-injection patterns and risky content are detected, and anything with legal or financial stakes always routes to a human.

Security & compliance

Answers ready before procurement asks.

DATA

Encrypted at rest, scoped per tenant

Mailbox OAuth tokens are sealed with authenticated encryption. Every customer row carries its organization id, and all access goes through tenant-scoped repositories.

ACCESS

SSO and least privilege

OIDC single sign-on with RS256 id_token verification. Three ranked roles (owner, admin, member) with no path to grant a role above your own.

RECORDS

Audit log, export, and erasure

Every security-relevant action is appended to a per-organization audit log. Owners can export their tenant's full data bundle, or purge it outright.
