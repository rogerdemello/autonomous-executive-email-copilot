CLASSIFY · SPAM

Junk never reaches you

Every message is classified as spam, normal, or urgent before anything else happens. Spam is filed with no reply and no human attention spent; the copilot never engages it, and the manufactured "urgency" of a promo email earns nothing.

  • Spam is labeled and filed automatically; it only ever adds a label.
  • The rationale is shown, so you can see exactly why it was filed.
  • Urgent mail is ranked to the top with a deadline attached.

DRAFT · EDIT · APPROVE

A professional reply, ready for your pen

For mail worth answering, the copilot writes the reply, fact-checks it against the source message, and waits. The draft opens in an editor; amend it freely before approving. Your edits are what get sent, and they teach the copilot your workspace's voice.

  • Model-drafted, with confidence shown next to the draft.
  • Verified against the source; invented numbers or names get flagged.
  • Nothing sends until you press "Approve & send".

RETRIEVE

Context before content

Before it acts, the copilot retrieves the thread's history and the sender's role, deadlines, and risk signals, so a reply answers the conversation, not just the last message.

REASON

Tools, not guesswork

The model acts through typed tools (classify, prioritize, reply, defer, escalate), each call carrying its reason and confidence, never free-form text parsed on hope.

VERIFY

Drafts are fact-checked

Every draft is read back against the message it answers. A number the source never said, or a name it never mentioned, is flagged and shown right beside the approve button.

GUARD

Injection-aware by default

Incoming mail is treated as untrusted input. Prompt-injection patterns and risky content are detected, and anything with legal or financial stakes always routes to a human.
